# Pass 1: Optics and Stock

Purpose: give the image a physical camera behind it, which counters the clean, weightless AI look. Every term is **"confirm per model"**: models differ in how much they respond to each, so test (`evals/cases.md`, case 9) and record results in `models/`.

Use terms in the Look anchor and in camera sentences. Pair each term with what it looks like when the shot size can show it.

## 1. Focal length and perspective

| Focal feel | 中文 | Look | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14–24 mm | 超广角 | Stretch, depth, edge distortion, environment dominates | Architecture, chase, disorientation; avoid on close faces |
| 28–35 mm | 广角 / 标准偏广 | Natural wide, shoulder-to-shoulder presence, slight room feel | Handheld drama, two-shots, walking scenes. 35 mm is the classic "cinema" default |
| 50 mm | 标准 | Neutral, close to a human view | Anything when you want no lens opinion |
| 85 mm | 中长焦 | Compressed face, flattering, clean separation from background | Portraits, intimate dialogue, tense close-ups |
| 100–135 mm | 长焦 | Stronger compression, isolated subject, background rises | Isolation, longing, surveillance feel |
| 200 mm+ | 超长焦 | Heavy compression, flattened layers, voyeuristic | Observed from far, crowds as texture |
| Macro / probe | 微距 | Extreme detail of eye, hand, object | Contact close-ups, ritual detail |

## 2. Aperture and depth of field

| Term | 中文 | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Wide open (T1.4–2.8), shallow depth of field | 大光圈浅景深 | Background melts to soft shapes, one plane sharp |
| Mid (T4–5.6) | 中等光圈 | Subject sharp with readable surroundings |
| Stopped down (T8+), deep focus | 小光圈深景深 | Foreground to background sharp; threat in layers |

Write the focus plane as a depiction: "the cup's rim stays sharp while Wu's face softens behind it." A rack focus needs new information to hand off to (a reveal, a reaction); avoid it mid-fast-action.

## 3. Lens character

| Character | 中文 | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Spherical, modern, clinical | 球面镜头，现代通透 | Clean, high micro-contrast |
| Vintage or softer glass | 老镜头 | Lower contrast, soft highlights, gentle halation |
| Anamorphic | 变形宽银幕 | Oval bokeh, horizontal streak flares, mild edge softness, wide frame (about 2.39:1) |
| Diffusion filter (Pro-Mist style) | 柔焦/柔光滤镜 | A faint glow around practical lights, softened highlights; also softens skin micro-detail |
| Vignette | 暗角 | Corners darker, subject drawn inward |
| Chromatic fringing | 色散 | Faint color edges at high-contrast borders, mostly at frame edges |
| Lens breathing | 对焦呼吸 | The image slightly changes scale as focus shifts |
| Practical flare | 光源眩光 | Streak or veil from a visible lamp, window, or flame |

## 4. Stock, sensor, grain

| Look | 中文 | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 35 mm film, tungsten-balanced stock (Kodak Vision3 500T style) | 35毫米胶片，钨丝灯平衡 | Warm practicals, visible grain in shadows, rich blacks |
| 35 mm film, daylight stock (250D style) | 日光胶片 | Cleaner daylight, finer grain, natural color |
| Eterna-style film | Eterna 风格 | Cooler greens, soft contrast, gentle skin |
| 16 mm | 16毫米 | Coarse grain, softer detail, documentary or period texture |
| 65 mm large format | 65毫米大画幅 | Very fine grain, huge detail and depth |
| Digital cinema sensor | 数字电影机 | Clean, wide dynamic range, grade carries the look |
| Fine, medium, coarse grain | 细/中/粗颗粒 | Grain lives in shadows and midtones, moves frame to frame; write it as film grain, never "noise" |
| Halation | 高光光晕 | A red-orange glow bleeding around bright highlights on film, best around lamps and neon |
| Highlight roll-off | 高光过渡 | Soft shoulders, no hard clipping |

Brand names are shorthand: always pair with the effect ("warm practicals, grain in the shadows") so a model that does not know the brand still gets the look.

## 5. Grade and color

| Term | 中文 | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Teal and amber | 青橙 | Cool backgrounds, warm skin |
| Bleach-bypass style | 漂白旁路 | Silvery, desaturated, high contrast, gritty |
| Low saturation, skin tones preserved | 低饱和保肤色 | Restrained palette, faces still read |
| Warm tungsten palette | 暖钨丝灯色调 | Amber highlights, deep warm shadows |
| Cool overcast palette | 阴天冷色调 | Blue-grey, flat soft contrast |
| Lifted blacks | 提黑 | Faded, filmic shadows |
| Crushed blacks | 压黑 | Dense, dramatic shadow |

Grade is not new content. It does not add objects or change locked facts.

## 6. Shutter, frame rate, motion rendering

| Term | 中文 | Look | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural motion blur (180° shutter feel) | 自然动态模糊 | Smooth, filmic motion | Default |
| Crisp, staccato motion (narrow shutter feel) | 清脆利落的动作（窄快门） | Little blur, choppy sharp movement | Combat, impact |
| Dreamy trailing blur | 拖尾模糊 | Smeared motion | Memory, drift |
| 24 fps cadence | 24帧电影节奏 | Filmic | Default |
| High-speed slow motion | 高速摄影慢动作 | Droplets, cloth, dust hang | Only on request, one beat |

## 7. Camera behavior

| Behavior | 中文 | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Locked-off tripod | 固定机位 | Stillness, observation, weight |
| Dolly in or out | 推轨 | Smooth approach or withdrawal, pressure or release |
| Slow push-in | 缓推 | Gradual intimacy or threat |
| Steadicam float | 斯坦尼康跟随 | Smooth following glide |
| Handheld with operator's breath | 手持，带呼吸感 | Small organic drift that settles when the subject stops |
| Whip pan | 甩镜 | Sudden redirect; landing must be identifiable |
| Crane or high rise | 升降 | Reveal of layout and scale |
| Orbit | 环绕 | Control, assessment; keep axis readable |

Motivate every move: write what causes it ("the camera drifts left as he stands", "the push-in starts when Wu stops speaking").

## 8. Look presets (starting points, adjust to the story)

| Scene | Look anchor |
|---|---|
| Intimate power dialogue | 85 mm, shallow depth of field, warm tungsten key, low-key shadows, fine grain, locked-off with a slow push-in |
| Gritty realistic combat | 35 mm handheld with operator's breath, narrow-shutter crisp motion, bleach-bypass desaturation, medium grain |
| Period wuxia, lantern night | Anamorphic feel, soft halation around flames, deep shadows, warm amber against cool blue moonlight, medium grain |
| Quiet interior melancholy | 50 mm, window light, low saturation with skin preserved, lifted blacks, fine grain, locked-off |
| Neon city noir | 35 mm, wet streets, halation around signs, teal and amber, fine grain, steady dolly |
| Stylized 2D or 3D animation | State the animation medium first; use virtual-lens terms sparingly (depth of field, bloom, subtle film grain overlay); skip stock names |

## 9. Contradictions to avoid

- Anamorphic and spherical in one look; fisheye and compression telephoto; shallow depth of field and "everything sharp, deep focus".
- Heavy diffusion filter while asking for visible pores.
- Narrow-shutter crispness with dreamy trailing blur.
- Handheld operator's breath and locked-off tripod in the same shot (a cut between them is fine).
- Day-bright grade with a night setting.
- More than three stock or grade terms; pick the dominant one.
