# Loading protocol

## 1. Primary route

Choose the actual deliverable: action (fight/chase), dialogue (spoken or silent interpersonal performance), visual (atmosphere/style/insert), ideation (gradual story exploration), review (actual output diagnosis). A moving fight containing a consequential exchange of dialogue uses both action and dialogue; merely grunting during contact does not load the whole dialogue library. A local edit reuses established facts and loads the owner of the changed dimension.

The [route manifest](../assets/routes.json) is the maintained read list. [read_route.py](../scripts/read_route.py) extracts original sections, deduplicates shared sections and prints counts. `--list` shows routes; `--stats` measures text only. Counts are workload observations, not creative targets or proof of reduced actual context use. No Python: read each listed exact heading manually.

## 2. Expansion without depth loss

After the primary route, account for each required responsibility: user targets; context; choices and causal chain; performance/listening; camera; look/material/environment/sound; continuity; actual input and platform conditions. A nonapplicable responsibility needs no elaboration. An applicable one needs a concrete solution, not merely a checked label.

Read an expansion when (a) a named move, nuance or look lacks a convincing design, (b) simultaneous tasks conflict, (c) references/assets or platform features alter execution, (d) continuation needs state, or (e) output becomes generic. Consult the [reference index](reference-index.md) for the matching owner/library. Complex action explicitly requiring travel and active camera expands spatial paths and camera rhythm. Concealment or close acting expands psychology and face. Reference-based style expands style translation. Known model input expands model evidence and generation handoff. Multi-clip work expands consistency and segments. Do not recursively load every link in those chapters; an unresolved responsibility triggers the next expansion.

## 3. Stopping and delivery

Stop expanding when each applicable responsibility has an executable design and no unresolved conflict blocks the requested deliverable. Preserve relevant descriptive richness; fewer reads do not authorize less action, weaker dialogue or generic texture. Write the complete requested draft; do not deliver a route plan in place of it. Review semantic target coverage before lint. Internal design records remain internal unless requested.

For exploratory ideation, preserve the wizard's concise state snapshot alongside its useful question or options; do not run final prompt modules prematurely. For output review, do not claim unseen motion or unheard sound. Existing user authorization determines whether to act, ask a consequential question or produce a labeled assumption.
