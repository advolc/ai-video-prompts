A fixed medium shot frames a narrow wooden doorway, the guard's waist and both hands. The guard wears a charcoal cotton coat and holds a short wooden staff across his chest. Soft daylight from the courtyard reveals the worn grain of the staff and the doorpost beside his shoulder.

A spear enters from the courtyard, its point driving toward his chest. He angles the staff into the wooden shaft just behind the spearhead. His elbows bend under the contact, then his hips turn as he guides the shaft outward toward the doorpost. The spearpoint passes beside his ribs, clear of his coat. A single dry knock marks the wooden shafts meeting. The staff remains against the displaced shaft; his torso still fills the doorway, with his weight held low behind the threshold. The camera stays still throughout the single parry. Quiet courtyard air continues beneath the brief contact sound.
